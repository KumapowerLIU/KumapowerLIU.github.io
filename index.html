I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at HKUST, advised by Prof. Qifeng Chen.

My research spans video generation and generative world models, human-centered multimodal spatial intelligence, and image generation and editing. I currently focus on post-training world models and embodied intelligence.

Outside the lab, I love fishing and hope to explore China’s rivers, lakes, and coastlines, one fishing trip at a time.
